Communication that Works for Real People

My work centers on language, literacy, accessibility, and user-centered design in educational and institutional contexts. I study and design communication that makes complex systems more navigable, supports multilingual communities, and turns everyday interactions into opportunities for learning.

Research Interests

Language & Literacy Development

I study how language is acquired, taught, and shaped across educational and community contexts, with attention to rural learners and first-generation students.

Accessibility & Inclusive Communication

My work emphasizes equitable design practices that improve clarity, usability, and access in digital, institutional, and pedagogical environments.

Multimodal & Rhetorical Circulation

I examine how texts, objects, and messages move across platforms and communities, shaping meaning through design, materiality, and form.
